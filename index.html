Metal / nonmetal / metalloid:

Each element has two cards: symbol → name and name → symbol. Get a card right and it comes back after a longer gap (1, 2, 4, then 7 days). Miss it and it comes back in a few cards, and again tomorrow. A review takes about 10 minutes. Up to 12 new cards are added per day.

Step by step: a new or missed card starts easy (pick the real spelling), then you build the name from pieces, then you type it with nothing to help, like the real quiz. Each right answer moves the card up a step. A card only counts as solid after you've typed it.

Spelling doesn't have to be perfect: a near miss counts, and you'll see the right spelling. In symbols the capital matters: Co (cobalt) is not CO.

Spelling families

The 42 names fall into a few families that share an ending. Learn the pattern once, then just the tricky letters (in red). Tap a name to see its symbol and where it lives. Names outside the element group you picked are faded.
